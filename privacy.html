Privacy Policy

Applies to Super Chess, CrossWars, FlipDeck, Battle Sudoku, Freestyle Scrabble, Salad Bowl, Crypt1C, Leave Lab, chesstrAIner and Rebus.
Last updated 17 August 2026.

The short version. These games have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Most of what the games store never leaves your phone. The multiplayer games send the minimum needed to play a game with the person you invited.

Who is responsible

These apps are made by one independent developer, Daniel Fogel. Questions, requests and abuse reports: dfogel89@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device only

Every game stores some things locally, using your device's app storage. This never leaves your phone, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • the display name you choose
  • your local win/loss record, game history and rating
  • saved games against the computer, and puzzle progress
  • your settings, including your chat filter preference and which players you have blocked
  • which one-time instruction popups you have already seen

What is sent to a server, and only in the multiplayer games

Super Chess, CrossWars, FlipDeck, Battle Sudoku, Freestyle Scrabble and Salad Bowl use Google Firebase Realtime Database so two phones can share one game. Crypt1C, Leave Lab, chesstrAIner and Rebus do not send any personal information at all — Rebus only downloads published puzzles, and the other three make no network requests for your data.

We do not ask for and do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or any advertising identifier. There is no sign-up and there is no account.

Chat and abuse reports

The games with chat let you report a message or a player. When you send a report, we receive the reason you chose, anything you typed, the messages you are reporting, and the anonymous IDs and display names of both people involved. Reports are stored privately — the person you reported cannot read them — and are used only to review what happened and act on it. See the Terms of Use for the chat rules themselves.

Blocking is separate and stays entirely on your device. We are not told who you blocked, and the person you blocked is not told either.

Who else can see any of this

Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising or any other commercial purpose. There are no analytics SDKs, no ad networks and no third-party trackers in any of these apps.

The only third parties involved are the infrastructure needed to run the games:

  • Google Firebase hosts the multiplayer database (Firebase privacy).
  • Expo delivers push notifications and app updates (Expo privacy).
  • Apple TestFlight and the App Store, and Google Play, distribute the apps and provide their own crash and install reporting to us in aggregate.

Children

These games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the chat in one of these games, email dfogel89@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. To remove everything stored on your device, uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings. To have game data, chat messages or reports associated with your games removed from the server, email dfogel89@gmail.com — include the four-digit game code or the display name you used, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Security

Traffic between the app and the database is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted by security rules: abuse reports cannot be read by anyone but us, and game rooms cannot be deleted or listed by players.

Being honest about the limit: a game room can be read by someone who knows or guesses its four-digit code. Do not put anything sensitive in the chat.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the updated version appears here.
