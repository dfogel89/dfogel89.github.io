Chat rules

Several of these games have in-game chat with the person you are playing. Before your first message the app asks you to accept these rules, and they apply every time you type:

  • No harassment, threats, or abuse.
  • No hate speech or slurs of any kind.
  • No sexual content, and nothing sexual involving minors — ever.
  • No spam, scams, or links to other services.
  • No sharing anyone's personal information, including your own.

The same standard applies to the display name you choose — it is shown to your opponent and counts as something you have posted.

Reporting and blocking

Every game with chat has a Safety button in the chat, and you can also tap any message your opponent sent. From either one you can report a message or a player, and block them. Blocking hides everything that person says, in every game on your device, and does not tell them.

Reports are reviewed and acted on. You can also email dfogel89@gmail.com directly.

What happens when someone breaks the rules

Depending on what happened, we may remove content, and may block a player from the multiplayer features or from the games entirely. Anything involving the sexual exploitation of children is reported to the appropriate authorities.
